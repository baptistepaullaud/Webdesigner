FAQ

Common questions

How long does an addition take?

Most single-storey additions take 10 to 14 weeks once the permit is issued. Second storeys take 16 to 22 weeks.

Do we have to move out?

Usually not. We seal the work area, protect the rest of the house and keep your kitchen and bathrooms working wherever possible.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We prepare the drawings, submit them and manage every inspection.

How is the price set?

After the site visit we send a written fixed price. It only changes if you ask us to change the scope.
